Privacy Notice

Effective date: November 14, 2026
Last updated: November 14, 2026

This notice explains what information Ebitdaa Ventures LLC ("we," "us") collects through ebitdaaventures.com (the "Site") and how we use it. It covers this Site only. Each of our products (for example Hojii, TenForma, Tidzee and WDYW?) has its own privacy policy, which applies when you use that product.

What we collect

Information you send us. When you use the contact form, we receive what you enter: your name, email address, phone number (optional), the topic you pick and your message.

Basic technical information. When you visit, the services that host the Site (GitHub Pages and Vercel) automatically receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages and time of your request, and may keep it in server logs to deliver and secure the Site. The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address and browser information when a page loads. We do not use analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking pixels on this Site.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message and follow up on your request.
  • To send you a one-time confirmation that we received your message.
  • To operate and secure the Site and prevent abuse.

We use your phone number only to respond to your inquiry. We do not add you to marketing texts or calls.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Site and reply to you, and only for that purpose:

  • Vercel (hosting and the function that processes the contact form);
  • Resend (email delivery of your message to us and your confirmation);
  • Microsoft (our Outlook business email, where your message is received);
  • GitHub (Site hosting); and
  • Google (font delivery).

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of our business, subject to this notice.

How long we keep it

Contact form messages are not stored in a database on the Site. A copy is kept in our business email for as long as we need it to respond and to keep ordinary business records, and in any case we delete it no later than 3 years after our last communication with you, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. Our email delivery provider (Resend) and hosting providers keep their own delivery and server logs for limited periods under their own retention settings. You can ask us to delete your message sooner (see Your choices).

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you sent us by emailing jordan@ebitdaaventures.com. We will respond within 45 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy law; contact us and we will honor requests as the law requires.

Do Not Track

Because the Site does not track you across other websites or over time, it does not change its behavior in response to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this notice. We will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date. If we make a material change to how we use information you already sent us, we will not apply it to that information without your consent.
